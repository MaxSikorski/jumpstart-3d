Step 1 · Pick the pieces

Pieces

    Anything you don't select stays on the backdrop, and the gaps left behind are filled in automatically. Standee is a flat cutout with a tab. Cross-slot is two copies that slot into an X, good for trees and towers. Use Duplicate or copy and paste to reuse a piece, like a row of trees.

    Step 2 · Check the build

    Drag a piece to move it forward or back. Drag empty space to turn the view, scroll to zoom. With a piece selected, the up and down arrow keys move it.
