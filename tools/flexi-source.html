  • Print as-is, flat on the bed, with no supports. All pieces and joints come off the printer already linked.
  • 0.2 mm layers suit the default 0.40 mm clearance. Use 0.45–0.5 mm if your printer tends to fuse gaps.
  • Overlapping, tab, ball and ring joints print one part over another across a small gap, like a support interface. Keep the clearance at two or more layer heights.
  • Free the joints after printing by bending each one gently until it moves smoothly.
