    How it converts

    • Coplanar triangles merge into single flat faces with straight edges.
    • Faceted holes, bosses and straight fillets become true cylinders. Countersinks, chamfers on round parts and tapers become true cones. Ball ends and domes become true spheres. Rounded edges around holes and bosses become true tori. Edges between them are exact circles and lines, so you can edit diameters, radii and axes.
    • Holes drilled at an angle, fillets where three rounded edges meet, and free-form areas stay faceted as small flat faces.
    • 3MF files keep their units, and each part becomes its own body in the STEP file.
    • Watertight meshes export as a solid body. Meshes with holes export as a surface body you can stitch in Fusion 360, Onshape or Inventor.
    • Works best on files exported from CAD. 3D scans have noisy surfaces and give many small faces.
