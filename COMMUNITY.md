# JumpStart 3D in the wild

Videos, posts and projects where people show JumpStart 3D. If you made
something, or covered the app somewhere, add it: open an issue at
https://github.com/MaxSikorski/jumpstart-3d/issues/new/choose and pick
"Share your content", or reply to the video or post with the link. We link
to your work; we do not copy it.

Newest first. Everything here is by the people named, not by this project.

## Where people talk about it

- [r/Jumpstart3D](https://www.reddit.com/r/Jumpstart3D/), a subreddit started by
  a community member in October 2026. Questions, prints, ideas.
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MaxSikorski/jumpstart-3d/discussions):
  announcements, polls, Q&A and Show and tell.

[![4 New 3D CAD/Modeling Programs!](https://img.youtube.com/vi/KGi11k9tQhA/hqdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/KGi11k9tQhA?t=308)

to 7:56. He tells the story of the original site disappearing and
this copy going back up, then walks through switching between Beginner

part starts (5:08 is 308), and `VIDEO_ID` is the part of the YouTube link
after `youtu.be/` or `v=`:
